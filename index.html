THE FORGOTTEN LIGHTNESS

CHRONICLES OF THE ARCANE WIZARD

For centuries, the realm flourished under the gentle radiance of the primordial light. But deep within the abyssal core, the Umbral Sovereign awakened, casting a smothering blanket of shadow across the lands.

As the sole surviving Arcane Adept, you reside in your secluded Forest Hut. To restore the Forgotten Lightness, you must plunge through 25 treacherous dungeon layers. Every 5 floors, deeper terrors awaken:

👹 F1-5: Void Goblins
🕷️ F6-10: Shadow Crawlers
🔮 F11-15: Spectral Cultists
🗿 F16-20: Abyssal Golems
⚔️ F21-25: Void Knights
👑 F26: Final Boss

Vanquish the Sovereign on Floor 26 to unlock Endless Mode and save the realm!
